# Art Direction

## Direction brief
Define a one-paragraph concept and a concise set of visual rules before implementation.

## Design dimensions
- **Composition:** editorial, modular, asymmetric, centered, split-screen, data-dense, immersive, etc.
- **Typography:** expressive display, neutral grotesk, humanist, serif-led, monospace accents, etc.
- **Color:** brand-led, restrained neutral, high-contrast functional, warm, cool, monochrome, etc.
- **Surface:** flat, outlined, layered, tactile, soft, industrial, etc.
- **Shape:** geometric, sharp, organic, rounded, mixed with purpose.
- **Imagery:** documentary, product photography, illustration, 3D, abstract, data visualization.
- **Motion:** functional feedback, guided transitions, narrative motion, or none.

## Guardrails
- Match direction to user, product, and context.
- Maintain readable contrast and clear hierarchy.
- Avoid decorative effects that obscure content.
- Use motion sparingly and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Do not imitate a living artist's exact style; describe broader visual attributes instead.
