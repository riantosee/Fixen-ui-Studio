# Content Design

- Write labels that describe the user's task.
- Use specific headings rather than vague marketing language.
- Keep primary actions verb-led and unambiguous.
- Explain units, dates, and status meanings.
- Prefer realistic sample content over lorem ipsum.
- Avoid unsupported claims, fake testimonials, invented metrics, and misleading urgency.
- Design for long names, localization, and empty data.
