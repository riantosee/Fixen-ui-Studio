# Code of Conduct

Fixen UI Studio welcomes respectful participation regardless of background or experience. Discuss ideas and evidence; do not attack people. Credit others' work and describe your results honestly.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, impersonation, malicious prompt injection, credential theft, unauthorized exploitation, and intentional disruption are prohibited in project spaces. Minimal inert examples for responsible defensive testing are allowed; live attacks and disclosure of secrets are not.

Maintainers may request corrections, remove harmful content, close contributions, warn participants, or restrict participation for repeated or severe misconduct. Decisions should be proportionate to impact. Do not retaliate against good-faith reports.

For security concerns, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For other sensitive conduct reports, use a private channel listed by [the maintainer](https://github.com/riantosee); if no channel exists, request one publicly without posting personal information. Maintainers also follow these standards.

These participation rules do not change the MIT license's grant of software rights.
